## Band gains

Each knob cuts or boosts by up to 12 dB. Frequency and filter shape are fixed.

| Band     | Frequency | Filter     |
| -------- | --------: | ---------- |
| Low      |    100 Hz | Low shelf  |
| Low mid  |    400 Hz | Bell       |
| High mid |     2 kHz | Bell       |
| High     |     8 kHz | High shelf |

Drag a knob or use the arrow keys. Press Home on a focused knob to reset it to 0 dB. The colored dots show the gain setting of each band, not the combined frequency response.

## Original and Prism

Select **Original** to bypass the EQ without losing your settings. Select **Prism** to hear the EQ again. Boosting bands adds level; leave some headroom when comparing.
