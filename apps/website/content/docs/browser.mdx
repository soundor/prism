## Choose a source

- **Synth loop:** Press Play below the plugin, then adjust the band gains.
- **Audio file:** Open the settings next to Play, select **Audio file**, and choose a file your browser can decode. The file stays on your device.

Use **Original** and **Prism** to compare the two sounds while the source plays.
