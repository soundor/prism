## Choose a source

Press Play below the plugin to hear the synth loop. To hear something else, open the settings next to Play and pick an **Input**:

- **Synth loop:** bass notes with a noise hat.
- **Drum loop:** kick, snare and hats at 120 BPM, for low end and transients.
- **Pink noise:** equal energy in every octave, in stereo, so mid and side bands both have something to work on. Set the analyzer's tilt to 3 dB/oct and it reads flat, so each band's shape shows clearly on the post trace.
- **Sine sweep:** a tone gliding from 20 Hz to 20 kHz every 8 seconds. Watch the meter and hear where a band boosts or cuts.
- **1 kHz sine:** a steady test tone.
- **Audio file:** choose a file your browser can decode. The file stays on your device.
- **Microphone:** your browser asks for permission first. Use headphones, or the speakers will feed back into the microphone.

Everything you hear goes through Prism's processing, as it would in a DAW.
