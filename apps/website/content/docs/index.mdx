title: Getting started
description: A 24-band parametric equalizer for VST3 hosts and the browser.
---

Prism is a parametric equalizer with up to 24 bands. You place and shape bands on a response graph, with a spectrum analyzer behind the curves, and fine-tune them in the inspector below it. A new instance has no bands, so it leaves your audio unchanged.

1. [Open the demo](/demo).
2. Double-click the graph to add a band, then drag it: across for frequency, up and down for gain. Scroll over it to change its width (Q).
3. Pick a factory preset from the menu at the top for a starting point. See [Controls](/docs/controls) for everything else.

## Install the VST3

Prism runs in DAWs that load VST3 effects on 64-bit Linux and Windows.
