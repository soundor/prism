# Branding

Soundor Prism is the four-band equalizer and companion website in the official
Soundor ecosystem. The [Soundor Brand Guidelines](https://soundor.dev/brand)
are the authoritative reference for the Soundor name and logos.

## The software is yours to use

Prism’s software is licensed under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3](LICENSE), Copyright (c) 2026-present
Dmitrii Balakin. These branding guidelines do not change what that license
permits.

## Referring to Soundor

You are welcome to:

- Describe your project as “Built with Soundor” or “Powered by Soundor”.
- Write about Soundor in documentation, tutorials, articles, and videos.
- Use the unmodified logo to link to Soundor or to illustrate something about
  Soundor itself.

## Keeping your project distinct

- Give your project or fork its own name and logo. Mention Soundor after its
  name: “Driftwood, an equalizer built with Soundor”, not “Soundor Driftwood”.
- Don’t imply your project is an official release, endorsed by Soundor, or
  affiliated with Soundor without permission.
- Don’t use the official logos or look-alikes as your product’s logo or icon.

The official Prism artwork is in [`assets/brand`](assets/brand). See the
[asset guide](assets/brand/README.md) for logo variants and usage notes.
For branding questions, [open an issue](https://github.com/soundor/soundor/issues).
